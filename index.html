Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about the Career Accelerator program, eligibility, commitments, and outcomes.

The SkillGenesis Career Accelerator is designed for ambitious college students, recent graduates, and early-career learners who want to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world employment. Whether your background is in engineering, management, humanities, or design, this accelerator equips you with practical, verifiable capabilities that employers actively hire for.
You will master in-demand industry skills including Applied AI and productivity workflows, data-informed business problem-solving, sales & growth strategy, and digital product execution. Beyond technical tools, you develop critical professional competencies: executive presentation, workplace communication, cross-functional collaboration, and decision-making under real project constraints.
Yes, absolutely. The Career Accelerator is thoughtfully structured specifically for students and working learners. Live mentor sessions and collaborative sprint reviews take place on weekday evenings and weekends. All resources, sprint templates, and project tasks are structured to seamlessly complement university commitments without overwhelming your academic schedule.
Yes. Building an authenticated portfolio is at the core of the program. Rather than superficial toy assignments, you will complete hands-on capstones modelled after genuine corporate initiatives. Every project comes with live documentation, code repositories, and measurable outcomes that you can confidently present to recruiters and hiring managers.
